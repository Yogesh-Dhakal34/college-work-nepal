🌋 Volcanic Eruptions

Experience the power of Earth's fiery forces.

Introduction

A volcanic eruption occurs when magma escapes through the Earth's crust, releasing lava, ash, rocks and gases. Some eruptions are gentle while others are explosive.

Major Causes

  • Tectonic plate movement
  • Magma pressure
  • Gas expansion
  • Hotspots
